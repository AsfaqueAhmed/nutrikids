# WHO growth data

LMS tables used by `apps/api/src/growth`. Generated by `pnpm --filter api exec ts-node scripts/import-who.ts`
from the raw WHO spreadsheets in `data/who/raw/` (git-ignored; re-download from the URLs below).

- 0-60 months: WHO Child Growth Standards, expanded tables (day-indexed, sampled at round(month x 30.4375) days).
  Head circumference is monthly. https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards
- 61-228 months: WHO 2007 growth reference for 5-19 years (height-for-age, BMI-for-age; weight-for-age to 120 months).
  https://www.who.int/tools/growth-reference-data-for-5to19-years/indicators
- Downloaded: 2026-10-18. **Provisional** until approved by a medical advisor.
